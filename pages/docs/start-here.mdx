June helps you understand how users interact with your product by tracking events, users, and companies.

## Core concepts

**Events** are actions that happen in your product, like signing up, making a purchase, or viewing a page.

<Flex my={8} justifyContent="center" align="center">
  <img alt="Track events" src={'/docs/event-explainer.png'} />
</Flex>

**Users** are the people using your product. Each user has a unique ID that lets you track their journey over time.

<Flex my={8} justifyContent="center" align="center">
  <img alt="Users profile" src={'/docs/users-explainer.png'} />
</Flex>

**Companies** are the organizations that your users belong to. Essential for B2B products where user behavior depends on their company context.

<Flex my={8} justifyContent="center" align="center">
  <img alt="Companies profile" src={'/docs/company-explainer.png'} />
</Flex>

**Traits** are additional properties about users and companies - like subscription plan, role, or company size. These help you segment and understand behavior patterns.

<Flex my={8} justifyContent="center" align="center">
  <img alt="Traits" src={'/docs/traits-explainer.png'} />
</Flex>
